Choose your beta route.

Mom Alarm Clock helps parents set child alarms, schedule everyday reminders, and see recorded status as phones sync.

Start with the phone you have

The iPhone beta is available through TestFlight for two iPhones. Android closed beta is for U.S. families with an Android parent phone and a child who has their own Android phone. Android sign-up has two observed steps; installation and two-phone functionality are still unverified.

Test on iPhone

Use TestFlight with two iPhones: one parent phone and one child phone. Install the latest build offered in TestFlight before testing.

Test on iPhone

This is beta software. Keep your usual backup alarm while you test.

Join Android closed beta

On each phone, use that phone’s own Play Store Google account. Each account must complete both steps separately: Step 1: join the Google Group. Step 2: opt into Play testing. Do not use the same Google account on both phones.

1. Join the Google Group 2. Opt into Play testing

If Play says “App not available,” check Group membership and the selected Google account; publication or region eligibility can also affect access. The Group page may show a permission error while still showing Join group near its title. Stay opted in for at least 14 days. Parent status can be delayed while phones sync. Keep a backup alarm. We observed the Play opt-in page, not an install or completed two-phone test.

Pair the two iPhones first

Set up the parent phone before testing alarms and reminders on the child phone.

  1. On the parent iPhone, create an account or sign in, then add the child.
  2. On the parent iPhone, show that child’s pairing QR code or pairing code.
  3. On the second iPhone, choose child-phone setup, scan the QR code or type the code, then assign that phone to the child.
  4. Set one alarm and one reminder, then check the recorded status after the phones sync.

Keep a backup alarm. Beta testing is useful, but it should not be the only way someone wakes up.

Tell us what happened

A screenshot gives the beta team a useful starting point. Do not include a child’s name or schedule in public comments.

  1. iPhone: take a screenshot in the beta app, tap its thumbnail, then tap Done. Choose Share Beta Feedback, describe what happened, and send it.
  2. Android: use the app’s Help & feedback option, or email momalarmclock@gmail.com with what happened.
