    What this preview is showing

    The simulator models the documented V2 product flow using the last reviewed native screenshots. Its visuals will be refreshed after the current app UI pass. It does not connect to a real device, schedule a real alarm, or collect your entries.

    Parent-managed alarmsChoose the time, wake-up check, message, and snooze allowance for the paired child device.
    Active wake checksThe child completes the configured check; an alarm merely firing is not treated as completion.
    Honest statusThe parent sees armed, ringing, checking, completed, and no-response states—not a vague success claim.
