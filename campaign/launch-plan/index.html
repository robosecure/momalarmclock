Proposed Founding 500 offer

HOLD — not live. This is a provisional internal strategy for Rob/Ross review, not a customer offer, reservation, countdown or call to action. Do not announce it until the required product, entitlement and terms work is verified.

The recommendation is to qualify the first 500 unique parent-owned family accounts that complete an eligible claim, rather than the first 500 downloads. The proposed benefit is the verified launch feature list free for the life of the app service, subject to exact published terms. Beta signups and Android Google Group signups alone do not reserve a Founding 500 spot.

Before any public claim, the team must freeze and verify the included feature list; build a server-atomic, one-per-family cap with an auditable claimed and remaining ledger; and prove Apple- and Google-store-compatible entitlement and restoration paths. A live remaining count must be truthful and come from that ledger. The exact Apple product, offer-code setup and review status still need validation; see Apple’s App Review Guidelines §3.1.1 and offer-code help.

The offer could help create early traction, while reducing later paid-conversion opportunity among qualifying families. That tradeoff needs measurement after a verified implementation; this page makes no performance prediction.

Before launch

  1. Verify a family can complete setup. Engineering must reproduce and resolve material pairing or alarm-creation problems with evidence. Confirm installation, pairing, parent alarm delivery, child wake-up check and the result recorded on the parent phone. Test reminder scheduling and the child/parent update separately.
  2. Capture the release that will be advertised. Record the exact version/build and matching screens. Give alarms and reminders equal useful examples, such as a morning alarm and taking out the trash or walking the dog. A recorded check or mark does not prove physical wakefulness or a chore being done.
  3. Finish and review the creative. Complete human listening, motion, caption and word-alignment QA plus current-build parity for the exact approved parent beta and GA cuts. Rework the two cartoon stories and obtain fresh exact review. Separately assemble and review the longer genuine-app film with release-matched footage, readable captions, both alarm and reminder benefits, and parent status qualified by synchronization. Historical clips are references only.
  4. Prove the destination and reporting. Check the store page, customer route, links and platform previews on a phone. Record report access and attribution definitions. Trial, paid conversion, revenue or refund values remain unknown when their sources show insufficient data.
  5. Prepare the exact paid setup while paused. Verify advertiser identity, country/device targeting, account currency/time zone, end date, native spending limits, fees and billing exposure. Keep the first test blocked if its released envelope or the overall ceiling cannot be contained when the operator is offline.

Free, accurate beta text and suitable community participation are authorized. Keep beta feedback in TestFlight’s screenshot/reporting flow, use demo details and avoid duplicate promotional posts. New media needs its exact review.

GA day: verify first, then release the reviewed work

  1. Open the live App Store destination and confirm the released version and advertised alarm/reminder behavior. Recheck the family setup path and customer links.
  2. Publish only the exact approved creative and placement. Update availability wording to what the store actually shows. Keep optional social follows separate from the primary product destination.
  3. If every activation condition passes, start up to $50 of funded tests with verified native limits. Check early delivery, destination and reporting; leave any unsupported experiment off.
  4. Record launch time, time zone, native campaign IDs, exact asset version, released amount and the first reporting window. Give delayed conversion reports time to settle before comparing outcomes.
A date does not activate a campaign. Pause for a wrong destination, material false claim, broken onboarding, suspicious traffic, missing measurement, overspend risk or inadequate account access. No guaranteed views, downloads, virality or profit.

Daily operations after launch

Listen and keep routes working

  • Check native comments, relevant messages, community moderation and TestFlight feedback. Respond helpfully from the owned brand identity.
  • Check destinations, current beta build availability and release wording. Confirm actual tester updates and completed tests separately from enrollment.
  • Record actionable problems, responsible owner and verification status. Keep private family and tester details out of public reports.

Reconcile before adjusting

  • Pull dated platform/store reports and separate reported spend from billing, fees, refunds and charges still pending.
  • Compare equivalent reporting windows and account time zones. Record unknown values and reporting delays.
  • Once tests are approved and active, Codex may adjust approved creative, search terms and placements within released channel and total caps. Log each change and next review; do not chase every small fluctuation.

Weekly decisions

  1. Report what changed, actual spend and available outcomes, with source, period, sample size and uncertainty. Separate organic reach, store downloads, activation, paid conversions and revenue.
  2. Review one controlled comparison at a time using approved variants that each include alarms and reminders. Compare a specific hook, format or placement with a comparable destination and audience; a newly made exact variant needs its own review.
  3. Keep, fix or stop an experiment using credible evidence. Weak clicks, downloads or family setup suggest different investigations, not automatic causal conclusions.
  4. Release small additional amounts only for a justified test or credible performer within the approved policy. New unapproved creative, platforms, increased caps or changed activation scope need review. Money may remain unspent.
