Before launch

  1. Verify a family can complete setup. Engineering must reproduce and resolve material pairing or alarm-creation problems with evidence. Confirm installation, pairing, parent alarm delivery, child wake-up check and the result recorded on the parent phone. Test reminder scheduling and the child/parent update separately.
  2. Capture the release that will be advertised. Record the exact version/build and matching screens. Give alarms and reminders equal useful examples, such as a morning alarm and taking out the trash or walking the dog. A recorded check or mark does not prove physical wakefulness or a chore being done.
  3. Finish and review the creative. Complete the approved combined cut’s listening and runtime checks. Finish the longer genuine-app film and any new versions with natural-speed narration, readable captions, both alarm and reminder benefits, and parent status updates qualified by synchronization. Retain historical clips as references; do not substitute outdated screens or unmade cuts for finished demonstrations.
  4. Prove the destination and reporting. Check the store page, customer route, links and platform previews on a phone. Record report access and attribution definitions. Trial, paid conversion, revenue or refund values remain unknown when their sources show insufficient data.
  5. Prepare the exact paid setup while paused. Verify advertiser identity, country/device targeting, account currency/time zone, end date, native spending limits, fees and billing exposure. Keep the first test blocked if its released envelope or the overall ceiling cannot be contained when the operator is offline.

Free, accurate beta text and suitable community participation are authorized. Keep beta feedback in TestFlight’s screenshot/reporting flow, use demo details and avoid duplicate promotional posts. New media needs its exact review.

GA day: verify first, then release the reviewed work

  1. Open the live App Store destination and confirm the released version and advertised alarm/reminder behavior. Recheck the family setup path and customer links.
  2. Publish only the exact approved creative and placement. Update availability wording to what the store actually shows. Keep optional social follows separate from the primary product destination.

A date does not activate a campaign. Pause for a wrong destination, material false claim, broken onboarding, suspicious traffic, missing measurement, overspend risk or inadequate account access. No guaranteed views, downloads, virality or profit.

Weekly decisions

  1. Report what changed, actual spend and available outcomes, with source, period, sample size and uncertainty. Separate organic reach, store downloads, activation, paid conversions and revenue.
  2. Review one controlled comparison at a time using approved variants that each include alarms and reminders. Compare a specific hook, format or placement with a comparable destination and audience; a newly made exact variant needs its own review.

  4. Release small additional amounts only for a justified test or credible performer within the approved policy. New unapproved creative, platforms, increased caps or changed activation scope need review. Money may remain unspent.
