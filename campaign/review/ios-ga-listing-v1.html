Description · 866 / 4,000 characters

Another trip to their room? Another reminder about the chores?

Mom Alarm Clock helps families coordinate morning alarms and everyday reminders across two iPhones.

From your iPhone, set an alarm on your child's phone and schedule reminders for everyday jobs. Your child receives the alarm and reminders on their iPhone.

When you set an alarm, choose how your child turns it off: answer a quiz, play a game, or scan a printed QR code.

When your child responds to an alarm or reminder, the response appears in Mom Alarm Clock. When the phones sync, you can see the alarm or reminder response on your iPhone.

Mom Alarm Clock shows how your child used the app. It cannot tell you whether they are out of bed or whether a chore is done.

Mom Alarm Clock is for parents who want a clearer way to manage the morning routine and everyday reminders from their own iPhone.
