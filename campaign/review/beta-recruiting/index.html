Owned-page post

Still going back to your child's room to wake them up? Mom Alarm Clock puts the alarm on their phone and the controls on yours. Set or change the alarm from your phone. To stop it, they hold a button, answer a question, play a quick game, or scan a printed code. If they're not up after the wait you chose, your phone is told.

We're looking for U.S. parents to test real mornings with two phones. This is a test build: keep a backup alarm for anything that matters. The parent's result can sometimes be slow to appear after the child finishes a wake-up check. Please stay in the beta for at least 14 days and tell us what goes wrong through Help & feedback in the app or momalarmclock@gmail.com. Please don't share a child's name or email in comments.

Landing-page copy

Set your child's alarm from your phone. See whether they actually got up.

The alarm rings on your child's phone. You set or change it on yours. To stop it, your child holds a button, answers a question, plays a quick game, or scans a printed code. If they aren't up after the wait you chose, your phone is told. The app does not read messages or show ads.

Android closed beta — U.S. parents with two Android phones

  1. Join the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/momalarmclock-beta
  2. Opt in and install on each phone using the same Google account that joined the group: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.momclock.momalarmclock

Each phone needs a group-joined Google account, including the child's phone. The install page may say the app is unavailable if you skip step 1 or open it under another account. Please stay opted in and in the group for at least 14 days, and use the app for real mornings on both phones.
