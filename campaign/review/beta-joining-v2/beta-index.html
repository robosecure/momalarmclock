Beta testing

Another trip to wake them up?

Set your child’s alarm from your phone. Their phone rings, and they finish a wake-up check to stop it. On iPhone, you can also schedule everyday reminders and check recorded status as phones sync.

Start with your phones

The iPhone beta is for two iPhones. The Android closed beta is for U.S. parents with their own Android phone and a child who has their own Android phone. Beta software can change; keep your usual backup alarm while you test.

Test on iPhone

Use TestFlight with a parent iPhone and a child iPhone. Install the latest build offered in TestFlight before testing.

Test on iPhone

Known iPhone beta problems: We are investigating problems stopping alarms in the app, and alarm edits can take several minutes to sync in the iPhone beta. Keep a backup alarm.

Join Android closed beta

On the parent phone, the selected Google account must join the Group first, then opt in with that same account in Play. Repeat those two steps on the child’s phone with that phone’s selected Google account.

1. Join the Google Group

If the Group page looks like a permission error, find Join group near its title.

2. Opt into Play testing

If Play says “App not available,” check the selected Google account and its Group membership and eligibility. If the child’s account cannot join, email support; do not try to work around account restrictions.

On the child’s phone, they finish a check by holding a button, answering a question, playing a quick game, or scanning a printed code to stop the alarm. Please stay opted in for 14 days and use it on real mornings. Android parent results can be slow to update. Keep a backup alarm.

Pair the two iPhones first

Set up the parent iPhone before testing alarms and iPhone reminders on the child iPhone.

  1. On the parent iPhone, create an account or sign in, then add the child.
  2. On the parent iPhone, show that child’s pairing QR code or pairing code.
  3. On the child iPhone, choose child-phone setup, scan the QR code or type the code, then assign that phone to the child.
  4. Set one alarm and one iPhone reminder, then check the recorded status after the phones sync.

Everyday reminders

On iPhone, schedule reminders and check recorded alarm and reminder status as phones sync.

Keep a backup alarm. Beta testing is useful, but it should not be the only way someone wakes up.

Tell us what happened

A screenshot gives the beta team a useful starting point. Do not include a child’s name or schedule in public comments.

  1. iPhone: take a screenshot in the beta app, tap its thumbnail, then tap Done. Choose Share Beta Feedback, describe what happened, and send it.
  2. Android: use the app’s Help & feedback option, or email momalarmclock@gmail.com with what happened.
