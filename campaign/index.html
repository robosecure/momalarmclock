Learn

A weekly loop that listens before it scales

The campaign should produce a short decision report, not a pile of platform numbers.

Each week

  • Collect App Store Connect, Apple Ads, manually checked native comments/inboxes, and momalarmclock@gmail.com feedback. The address is not verified as an automatic alert for every social comment; continue manual checks until a controlled alert-and-inbox test succeeds for each platform.
  • Separate platform delivery, App Store clicks, reported new downloads, campaign-link observations, setup/activation evidence, and complaints.
  • Review the exact creative, destination, audience/territory, and feedback themes; record what remains unknown. Do not solicit or reward ratings or stars.

Decision report

  • Pause or fix when delivery is wrong, the store path breaks, spend outruns verified installs, setup or reliability fails, or feedback identifies misleading creative.
  • Scale gradually only after reported new downloads plus aggregate activation/return hold as spend rises.
  • Views, clicks, shares, and referrals can diagnose reach; they do not alone justify more spend. “Viral” means unusually self-propagating sharing, not a result this $500 learning test can promise. There is no viral coefficient or revenue claim until instrumentation supports one.
